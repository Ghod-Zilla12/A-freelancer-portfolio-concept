3 Case Studies
01 • Packaging & Brand Identity

Eko Roasters Coffee Co.

The Problem

Generic kraft pouches blended into supermarket shelves, causing low repeat retail orders.

What I Did

Designed a bold custom wordmark, color-coded roast labels, and print-ready pouch templates in 10 days.

Result: Retail stockist orders increased by 45% within 6 weeks of launch.

02 • Logo Suite & Social Launch Kit

Zuri Glow Skincare

The Problem

Inconsistent Instagram graphics and an unreadable script logo hurt customer trust at checkout.

What I Did

Created a clean geometric emblem, 3-color palette, product labels, and 15 reusable Canva/Figma social templates.

Result: Instagram profile-to-WhatsApp inquiries rose by 60% in the first month.

03 • Delivery Fleet & Menu Redesign

SwiftBite Lagos

The Problem

Cluttered paper menus confused lunch customers and slowed down WhatsApp order confirmations.

What I Did

Redesigned a scannable one-page digital menu, rider box decals, and sticker seals across 3 outlets.

Result: Average order decision time dropped by 35% and combo meal sales grew by 28%.
